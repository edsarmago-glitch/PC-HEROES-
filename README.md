Kuro-Kuro is an apparel brand proudly based in Marikina.

The brand name comes from the Tagalog word “kúro-kurò”, or in English, “personal opinion”. Kúro-kurò the brand is mostly known for its cynical but earnest statement tees, catering to the Filipino youth of the metro.

Kúro-kurò’s visuals are heavily inspired by the street art of its hometown, and seasonally collaborates with local artists and designers for its products. [EXTRACTED FROM HOME PAGE]
